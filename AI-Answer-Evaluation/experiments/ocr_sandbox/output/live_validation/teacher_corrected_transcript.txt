Unit: IV
Woodland Whispers
Chapter: 1
Shakuntalam
Summary
The play Shakuntalam written by
Kalidasa vividly pictures how our ancestors
lived in harmony with mother nature. The
play shows us how we can co-exist with
nature. The play begins with chasing a deer
in the forest. His charioteer drives the
king chariot. The king admires the beauty of the deer.
He is about to shoot the deer. Then a voice
warns him not to kill it. The deer belonged
to a hermitage. The two hermits appeared
there and explained that the deer is protected.
They tell him that weapons should be used
to protect the innocent and not to harm
the weak. The king respect their words. The
hermits bless him. They invite him to Sage Kanva.
